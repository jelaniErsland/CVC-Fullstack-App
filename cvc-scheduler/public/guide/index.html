02 · FIRST THINGS FIRST

Getting signed in

As a project contact, you have your own secure sign-in. No password to create or remember.

1

Open the contact sign-in page

Visit projectlocal.app/admin/login. This is different from the personalized schedule links volunteers receive.

2

Enter your contact email

Use the email address associated with your project-contact account. You'll receive a secure link in your inbox. Check spam or junk if it doesn't arrive.

3

Open your link

The link signs you in to your own account, with access to the areas you're authorized to use. Please don't forward it or share your account with someone else.

✦ If a helper needs access, contact Jelani so they can be given their own account.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Go directly to your sign-in page

Use the email address linked to your project-contact account. The app will send you a secure sign-in link.

Need access first? Ask Jelani to set up your account.

What appears here depends on the current project and its schedule.

04 · THE HEART OF THE APP

Calendar & assignments

Most of our daily coordination will happen here. You can view the work by day, week, month, or list, and schedule general help, Food, Security, and other tasks using the same Calendar.

!
One important distinction

Save → Publish → Send. Saving an assignment doesn't automatically email the volunteer. Ordinary new work begins as a private draft; publish it when the schedule is ready, then use Communications to email the volunteers.

Choose what you're trying to do

One day of work

01 Open Calendar and choose Create item.

02 Choose an existing Task or create a custom item, then select One date and enter the time and needed volunteers.

03 Choose the volunteers, review the summary, and select Create item.

04 Publish the item when you're ready for volunteers to see it. Email them separately through Communications.

Several days, one save

01 Choose Create item, then use Repeat to select the date range and weekdays. Those controls determine the scheduled days.

02 Under Assign volunteers, select the people you need. By default, they're included on every scheduled day.

03 If someone can't make one of those days, open Adjust individual days and select the days to skip for that person.

04 Check the live summary and choose Create items once. You can publish the new work after reviewing it.

EXAMPLEMonday–Friday · 3 volunteers

If Riley can't make Tuesday, exclude Tuesday for Riley. The other volunteers remain assigned to all five days.

The work is already scheduled

01 Click the Calendar item you want to assign. That item is selected automatically.

02 To include other existing days, check Also assign on other dates and choose the additional matching items. Nothing else is selected automatically.

03 Choose your volunteers. Use Adjust individual days only if someone won't be there on every selected date.

04 Review the summary and choose Save assignments. Previously existing assignments are skipped rather than duplicated.

05 · KEEP THINGS CONSISTENT

Tasks

A Task is the type of work; a Calendar item is that work scheduled for a specific date and time. Reusing Tasks keeps our Calendar recognizable.

01 Go to Tasks to see what's already available. Each Task has its own color and may include default information.

02 When scheduling, choose the existing Task that fits rather than creating several versions of essentially the same job.

03 If you genuinely need a new type of work, create a Task so it can be reused later.

✦ General help, Security and Food all use the same underlying Calendar system.

06 · OUR SHARED ROSTER

Volunteers

This is where we can find volunteers, add new people, and maintain the information that helps us arrange their support.

01 Open Volunteers to search the roster, open a profile, or add a new volunteer.

02 Keep contact details, availability, skills, and other relevant information current. Use private fields such as emergency contacts only when authorized and necessary.

03 If you have a spreadsheet, choose the Import CSV option. Review the matching and field selections carefully before saving, especially where a person may already have a profile.

04 Ordinary CSV exports exclude private date-of-birth and emergency fields. Private exports require additional authorization and an explicit acknowledgment.

BEFORE YOU IMPORT

Check for existing profiles

The import tool is there to save time, but please don't merge uncertain matches or overwrite someone's information without reviewing the proposed changes. Blank spreadsheet cells won't automatically erase their saved values.

Adding or importing someone never sends them an email automatically.

07 · KEEP EVERYONE INFORMED

Communications

Once the schedule is ready, this is where we introduce volunteers to the app and email them their assignments. Every send requires recipient review and your explicit confirmation.

01

Welcome emails

New volunteers who haven't received an introduction appear here. Review who should receive one, then confirm the operation. Creating a profile does not trigger an automatic email.

02

Schedule emails

Choose the date range and review volunteers with new, unsent, published assignments. Each person can receive one email with their own schedule for that period.

03

Delivery history

See what has been sent, review the outcome, and explicitly resend when necessary. A known failed delivery can be retried; an uncertain outcome needs investigation first.

✦
A quick reminder

Schedule emails and introductory emails are separate. Please coordinate any large initial email batches with Jelani, and double-check volunteers who share an email address before sending.

08 · SEE WHAT NEEDS FOLLOW-UP

Needs Attention

This page brings together staffing and response issues, such as declined assignments or work that still needs additional help.

01 Open Needs Attention to review your current follow-up signals.

02 Open an item for its details, then follow up with the volunteer or make the necessary Calendar adjustment.

03 Mark it reviewed when you've personally looked into it. Your reviewed/unreviewed status is independent of the other contacts.

✦ It's worth checking this regularly, especially for the assignments you're responsible for overseeing.

09 · DAILY OPERATIONS

Food & Security

These are scheduled through the same Calendar as general volunteer work, rather than a separate spreadsheet or scheduling system.

Food

Breakfast and Lunch are separate Calendar items. The appropriate contacts can maintain their meal details, including providers, menus and their respective meal totals. These totals remain separate.

Security

Security shifts can be scheduled and assigned in the normal Calendar workflow. Volunteers then confirm or decline through their personal schedules, just as they do with other assignments.

Volunteers can see published menu information without seeing private Food-contact notes.

10 · SEE WITHOUT CHANGING

Quick View

Quick View is useful for authorized people who need to see the project Calendar but shouldn't be changing assignments or other scheduling information.

It uses a read-only view of the project's published operational Calendar. Where authorized, this includes assigned volunteers and the relevant Food and Security details.

When someone needs Quick View access, use the app's intended sharing controls. Please don't share your own administrative sign-in link or forward somebody else's private schedule link.

11 · FROM THE VOLUNTEER'S SIDE

What volunteers see

Volunteers don't need an administrative account. For the current beta, they access their schedule using the personalized secure link sent to their email. Don't direct volunteers to name or contact-information lookup unless that feature has been enabled and verified in the deployed app.

✓

Review assignments and respond. They can Confirm, choose Can't make it, or confirm several eligible assignments at once.

☷

See the lunch menu. Published weekly meal information is available even for days when they aren't personally assigned.

↗

Record away dates. This alerts us to possible conflicts but doesn't automatically decline or cancel any existing assignment.

✦ If someone tells you they can't make an assignment, encourage them to respond in the app when possible so everyone can see the current response.

12 · A FEW OTHER QUESTIONS

Good to know

Some of the questions that are likely to come up as we begin using the app together.

Does adding a volunteer automatically email them?

No. Adding or importing a profile doesn't automatically send a welcome or schedule email. You choose when to send these from Communications.

What if I assign someone to several days?

You can create repeated work or choose additional existing Calendar items, then select your volunteers once. Each day still has its own independent assignment and response. One schedule email can cover the selected period.

What if someone says they can't make it?

Encourage them to select Can't make it on their own schedule if possible. You can then follow up and change their assignment if needed. Check Needs Attention for relevant signals.

Does entering an away period remove existing assignments?

No. It records availability and shows potential conflicts, but it doesn't automatically cancel or decline work that was already assigned.

What if someone else needs access?

Contact Jelani to arrange their own project-contact account, or use Quick View if they only need authorized read-only Calendar access. Never share your personal sign-in or another volunteer's schedule link.
